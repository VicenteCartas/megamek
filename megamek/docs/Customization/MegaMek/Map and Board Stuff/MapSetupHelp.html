Setting Up the Game Map

The map setup has two halves: the war table on the left shows the game map's board slots as the boards they hold, and the board drawer on the right lists the boards you can put in them.

Map Type and Size

  • Ground map, Low altitude map or Space map at the top chooses the kind of map.
  • Boards across and Boards down set how many boards make up the map.
  • Board size sets the size every board of the map shares. Choose Custom board size to enter your own.
  • A space map is one open board: you only set its size, and the drawer, the board slots and the random map settings do not apply.

Filling the Board Slots

  • Click a slot on the war table to pick it. Ctrl-click picks several slots at once.
  • Pick a board in the drawer and press Place, or double-click it, to put it in the picked slots.
  • Add to stack adds the picked boards to the slots' stacks as alternatives, keeping the board each slot shows now. Ctrl-click in the drawer picks several boards to add at once.
  • Generated, first in the drawer, makes a slot a generated board: the game creates it when the game starts, from the random map settings. The picture is only an example. Regenerate rolls new examples, and you can change the settings with Random map settings at the top.

Stacks and Surprise Boards

  • A slot with several boards shows a stack button. Open it to see the boards, choose the one shown, or remove one from the stack.
  • The roll button reveals another random board from the stack.
  • The surprise button hides the board: one of the stack's boards is chosen at random when the game starts.

Each Slot's Controls

  • The turn button rotates the board by 180 degrees. It works only for fixed boards with an even width.
  • Theme draws the board in another terrain theme, such as snow or Mars.
  • Level raises or lowers every hex of the board; the reset button restores the original heights.

Finding Boards

  • Search boards looks through the boards' names and tags once you type three letters. Separate several terms with ; to find boards that match all of them.
  • Tags and Folders show only the boards with the checked tags or from the checked folders. Match all tags asks for boards that have every checked tag rather than any of them. The active filters show as chips under the bar: click a chip to remove it, or Clear filters to remove them all.
  • Any size, on a one-board map, lists the boards of every size, with width and height ranges to narrow them. Placing such a board makes it the whole map at its own size.
  • The button at the top right of the drawer switches between thumbnails and a list.
  • Preview on a board shows it in 3D in place of the war table, to look around before you place it; Back to layout returns.

Saving and Previewing

  • Load map setup and Save map setup keep a whole map layout for later. For a generated board, only the fact that it is generated is saved, not the board shown.
  • Board Editor opens the board editor to make or change boards.
  • Preview at the foot of the war table shows the whole assembled map in 3D.
  • The settings button at the top opens the planetary conditions.
